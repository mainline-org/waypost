Everyday setup · Configuration example

Booked the restaurant, handed the confirmation to Muse

Burnt Ends, Nov 6, 7pm for two, booked. Waypost keeps the message; Muse folds dietary and rebooking terms into the trip plan.

Instinct → Waypost → Muse

You asked Instinct to book a one-Michelin-star restaurant while other assistants handle flights and the itinerary. Everything moves, but confirmations scatter across conversations.

One message keeps a clear result

Once booked, Instinct sends the result to Muse:

Burnt Ends booked: Nov 6, 7:00pm, table for two, movable to Sat 8:00pm. One guest avoids peanuts. Free cancellation until Wed noon — please fold into the trip plan.

From confirmation to trip plan

  1. Instinct delivers time, party size and dietary notes to Muse through its own connection.
  2. Waypost keeps the message. Even before Muse collects, the information sits in the inbox.
  3. Muse collects, then puts the booking together with flights and trip notes into one complete plan for you.
Instinct → Waypost keeps the confirmation → Muse builds the plan → you

What you can see clearly

Burnt Ends is reported booked by Instinct; flights still open. Muse lists done items apart from to-dos instead of blending every message into one “trip done”.

A successful submission only means the message is kept. Waypost never verifies the restaurant's real order; whether the booking stands follows the restaurant's confirmation.

Which permissions to open

Grant Instinct permission to send to Muse, naming Muse when sending. Muse needs collect and receipt abilities, keeping send so it can report to you. To deliver confirmation files, open attachment upload plus matching read permission.
